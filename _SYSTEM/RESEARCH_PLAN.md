# RESEARCH PLAN — The AI Engineering Brain

## Mission

A completely interconnected Obsidian vault covering AI engineering **top to bottom**: from surface-level orientation to impossibly niche unicorn knowledge. Real mechanisms, real numbers, real failure modes, real tribal knowledge — zero listicle content. Every topic that can connect to another, does.

Companion documents:
- `_SYSTEM/STANDARDS.md` — content types, formats, linking law, quality contract. **The constitution. Read first.**
- `_SYSTEM/NOTE_REGISTRY.md` — the frozen inventory of every note title. The only valid link targets.
- `_SYSTEM/PROGRESS.md` — build state across sessions.

## Architecture

19 content domains + Home. Each domain **owns** its topics exclusively — other domains link to them rather than re-explaining. Ownership disputes are settled by the boundary table below; when in doubt, one domain owns the mechanism and the other links to it from its own angle.

### Anti-overlap contract
1. A topic has exactly one owning domain (table above). Writers never re-explain owned material — they link.
2. Architects design only their domain; boundary column is binding.
3. Writers may only write the notes assigned to them, using registry titles for every link.
4. Cross-domain seams are linked from *both* sides (Linking Law §5.8).

### Research quality directive for all agents
Depth comes from mechanism-level knowledge of real systems and papers — the kind found in engineering blogs (vLLM, Anthropic, DeepSeek papers, EleutherAI), lab logbooks, and framework source code — not from generic tutorials. When you know the specific number, name, or incident, state it. When something is folklore, label it folklore and keep it (labeled folklore IS unicorn knowledge). Never pad; never generalize away the detail that makes knowledge real.
